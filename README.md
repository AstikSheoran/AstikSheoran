## About

I'm a full stack engineer who owns features from the first Figma frame to the production deploy. Right now I'm a **Senior Developer at Way2Class**, where I make the architectural calls on production applications — the kind of decisions you have to live with for years, not sprints.

- 🏗️ I build with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Node.js, MongoDB and Firebase**
- 🚀 I care about **scalable architecture, performance, and long-term maintainability** — not just shipping
- 🤖 I work fluently with **AI-assisted development** (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot, MCP) and ship AI features into real products
- 🎓 **B.Tech in Computer Science** — University Institute of Engineering & Technology (UIET), MDU
- 📍 Based in **Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I started out with a **Diploma in Civil Engineering** before switching to software. Turns out both are about foundations.

`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Firebase`

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Firebase` `Groq AI`
